How it works

Move. Snap. Play!

Stop motion is a magic trick made of tiny moves. Take a picture, move something a little, take another. Play them fast, and your toys walk, jump and fly.

  1. 1🧸

    Move

    Put a toy, a puppet or some clay in front of the camera. Move it just a little.

  2. 2📸

    Snap

    Press SNAP! to take a picture. The ghost shows your last picture, so you can see how far you moved.

  3. 3🍿

    Play

    Move, snap, move, snap. Press Play any time and watch it come alive.

  4. 4🎉

    Done

    Press Done twice and your movie is saved to Photos. Want words, sounds and songs first? Tap the pink magic wand to decorate it.

SNAP! Oops Play Ghost Done

Five big buttons. One job each.

  • Snap takes a picture.
  • Oops takes back the last picture.
  • Play plays the movie so far.
  • Ghost shows the last picture faintly over the camera, or hides it.
  • Done, pressed twice, saves the movie to Photos and starts a new one.

After the first picture, the camera locks its focus, exposure and white balance, so every picture matches and the movie doesn't flicker. Pick a speed: 🐢 Slow, 🐇 Medium or 🚀 Fast, 6, 12 or 24 pictures a second.

✋ Auto Snap

Hands off the phone.

Move something, take your hands away, and Auto Snap takes the picture once everything holds still, then plays the camera sound. Nobody has to touch the phone, so it never wobbles.

It won't take the same picture twice, and it waits while a hand is still in the picture. The hand check runs on your iPhone with Apple's Vision. Snap and Oops keep working the whole time.

Classrooms, libraries, museums and parties

Put the stage on the big screen.

Inspired by the puppet stations at children's museums. Plug in a TV and a box of big buttons, and anyone can walk up and make a movie.

  • 📺

    The TV is the stage

    Connect a TV or monitor with a USB-C to HDMI adapter, or use AirPlay. The TV shows the live picture, the ghost and playback, and the phone becomes the remote.

  • 🕹️

    Any button will do

    Keyboards, programmable button boxes, foot pedals, game controllers (including the Xbox Adaptive Controller), Bluetooth camera remotes, the volume buttons, Camera Control and AirPods.

  • 🏛️

    Museum mode

    Hides the movie library and photo import, and opens straight into a fresh movie. Turn on Guided Access and the phone stays in the app.

  • 🎉

    Next kid's turn

    Kids press Done twice. The movie plays on the TV while it saves to Photos, then a fresh movie starts.
